# Code of Conduct

## We Build Together

**FOV (Flexible Output View)** is built by a small core team and community with one goal: giving viewers ultimate control over their streaming experience.

This Code of Conduct applies to all repositories under the `Flexible-Output-View` organization (`obs-studio-fov`, `web-fov`, `documentation-fov`), as well as our associated communication channels (GitHub Discussions, issue trackers, and community spaces).

---

## Our Values & Standards

*   **Respectful collaboration:** We value diverse perspectives, open discussion, and mutual respect.
*   **Constructive feedback:** Critique ideas, not people. Be direct, kind, and professional.
*   **Quality over speed:** We ship stable software, not rushed code, prioritizing reliability and care.
*   **Transparency:** Our GitHub Discussions and public channels are the primary places for community input and architectural ideas.

### Unacceptable Behavior

To ensure a welcoming, safe, and professional environment, the following behaviors are strictly unacceptable across our repositories and spaces:
*   Unwelcome sexual attention, language, or imagery.
*   Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
*   Public or private harassment, bullying, or intimidation.
*   Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.


## Disagreements & Governance

We resolve technical and project-related disagreements by **majority decision** within the core team (as outlined in our [Contribution Guidelines](./contributing.md)). Keep discussions constructive, professional, and focused on what's best for FOV and its users.


## Reporting Issues & Enforcement

The core team is responsible for enforcing these standards and holds the authority to remove inappropriate comments, contributions, or ban repeat/severe violators from organization spaces.

*   **Public Feedback:** For general project feedback, ideas, or questions, please use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Flexible-Output-View/discussions).
*   **Safety Reports:** If you encounter harassment, abuse, or safety violations that require confidential intervention, please contact the core team [@LucasLoustalot](https://github.com/LucasLoustalot), [@RaphxelS](https://github.com/RaphxelS) or [@Slymoz](https://github.com/Slymoz).
